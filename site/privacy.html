Privacy notice

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

You can use the Amharic App without an account. Then your progress stays in your browser on your device, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

If you choose to sign in, your email address and your learning progress are stored with Google Firebase so you can carry on from any device. Nothing else is collected, there are no adverts and no tracking, and you can delete it all from the app at any time.

Who is responsible

The Amharic App (amharicapp.com) is run by Robert Sadler in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the information below. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

Using the app without an account

Your profiles, scores, settings and any recordings of your own voice are kept in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them, so use Profiles › Back up if you want a copy.

What is stored if you sign in

  • Your account: your email address, a random account ID, and when you signed up and last signed in. If you sign in with Google, Google also passes on your name and profile picture, but the app doesn't save or use them.
  • Your progress: the profile you linked to your account, which means its name, test scores, the words and letters you've practised and when, your mistakes list, your daily goal and streak, and your app settings (such as transcription on or off, or audio speed).

Your recordings of your own voice are never uploaded. Other profiles on the same device stay on the device.

Why, and on what basis

This information is used only to sign you in and to keep your progress the same on all your devices. The legal basis is that you asked for this service by creating an account (contract, UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). It is never sold, shared for marketing, or used for adverts.

Where it is kept, and who else handles it

Accounts and progress are held by Google's Firebase service (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us under Google's data processing terms. Progress is stored in Google's London data centre. Google may handle sign-in data in other countries, including the United States, under the safeguards in its terms. See Firebase's privacy information.

The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load the page. Fonts come from Google Fonts. The "Support this app" button takes you to Ko-fi, which has its own privacy policy.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. If an account isn't used for three years, it may be deleted.

Deleting your data, and your other rights

To delete everything: open Profiles, and under Your account tap Delete my account. This removes your account and the progress saved in it straight away. Progress on your device is not touched. Signing out stops syncing but keeps your account.

You also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, or to object to how it is used. Email the contact above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and browser storage

The app uses your browser's storage to keep your progress and settings, and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. This storage is needed for the app to work, so there is no cookie banner. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Children

The app can be used by anyone without an account. Accounts are meant for people aged 13 or over. Younger learners can use a profile on a parent's device, or a parent can sign in for them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change too.
